1. Who we are

Litefy (the "App") is developed and published by Zareen Labs ("we", "us", "our"), based in Pakistan. This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Litefy apps for iOS and Android.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

Images and files you process

All compression, conversion, and editing happens locally on your device. We do not upload, view, store, or have access to your images or files. Processed files are saved only where you choose on your device.

Information stored on your device

The App stores your conversion history, settings (such as theme and quality preferences), and usage counts (such as the number of free compressions used) on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear history or uninstall the App.

Account information (optional sign-in)

If you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive the information that provider shares with us, which may include your name, email address, profile picture, and a unique account ID. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead of your real email. We use this only to identify your account and link your subscription. We do not receive your password.

Purchase and subscription information

When you start a trial or subscribe, the payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receive purchase details such as product, price, purchase date, trial and renewal status, an anonymous app user ID, and limited device information (such as OS version, app version, and country). We never receive your card or payment details.

Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message, and use it only to respond to you.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising ID, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed above, to run the App;
  • if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users or others;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your data.

8. Data retention

  • Images and files: never received by us.
  • On-device history and settings: kept on your device until you clear them or uninstall the App.
  • Account information: kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes.
  • Support emails: kept for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved.

9. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the App: go to Settings › Account › Delete account.
  • By email: send a request to abdullahzareen901@gmail.com with the subject "Litefy data deletion request" from the email linked to your account. We will complete your request within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. To stop billing, cancel it in your Apple ID settings (iOS) or Google Play subscriptions (Android). To remove on-device data, clear history in the App or uninstall it.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Litefy from Facebook › Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and websites.

11. Security

Because your images are processed on your device, they never travel over the internet to us. Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your information.

12. Children
